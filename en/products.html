Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy Tantie Lorraine's jams in Portugal and Spain?

Sales in Portugal and Spain open soon. To be notified, send us an information request.

Which products are available in Portugal and Spain?

Four chilli jams, Scotch Bonnet Jam (SBJ), Apple Chilli Jam (ACJ), Red Pepper Chilli Lime Jam (RPCL) and Tomato Chilli Jam (TCJ), and a chilli jelly, Scorpion Jelly (SCO).

Which is the mildest jam?

Apple Chilli Jam: sweet, fruity and gently warm, for those who want the flavour of chilli without too much heat. Red Pepper Chilli Lime Jam and Tomato Chilli Jam have medium heat. Scotch Bonnet Jam is made with fermented Scotch Bonnet chilli mash (100,000 to 350,000 Scoville heat units). Scorpion Jelly, made with Trinidad Moruga Scorpion chillies (up to 2,009,231 Scoville heat units), is the hottest in the range.

What do you eat these jams with?

Tomato Chilli Jam was made for cheese and charcuterie boards. The brand also suggests sandwiches, such as egg mayonnaise, rocket and Scotch Bonnet Jam, or pesto, cheese, rocket and Tomato Chilli Jam. There are more ideas in Posts.

Where are they made?

By hand, in small batches, in Surrey, London, with recipes from Trinidad and Tobago, the homeland of the founder, Lorraine.

Have the jams won awards?

Scotch Bonnet Jam received two stars at the 2024 Great Taste Awards, run by the Guild of Fine Food. The brand also holds Great Taste awards from 2019, 2021 and 2026.

Do the jams contain allergens?

Yes: all four jams contain sulphur dioxide and sulphites. The ingredients and nutrition information for each jam are on this page.

Who represents the brand in Portugal and Spain?

GADGETMÉTODO, LDA, based in Porto. Contact: info@tantielorraine.pt.
